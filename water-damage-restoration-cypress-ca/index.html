What water damage looks like in Cypress homes

Cypress was incorporated in 1956 as Dairy City, and it was once nicknamed Waterville for its many artesian wells. The dairies sold to developers in the 1960s and none remained by the 1970s, so most Cypress neighborhoods were built within a few decades of each other.

That history is context rather than a forecast for your street, but it points to a practical step. What to do before we arrive in Cypress if water is still spreading

Close the valve

Water in Cypress is supplied by Golden State Water. Your bill confirms the provider, and the main shutoff in your house stops most leaks.

Power and wet areas

Cut power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. If water touches outlets, call the utility or 911 first.

Lift and protect

Move rugs, electronics and paperwork to dry shelves. Use foil or blocks under furniture legs.

People and pets

Keep everyone out of water from a toilet, drain or outdoors. It may be contaminated.
