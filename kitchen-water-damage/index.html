Where kitchen leaks start and where the water ends up

Kitchens have more water connections than any room except the bathroom. The dishwasher, sink supply and drain, ice maker line, filter and disposal all sit inside or against cabinets, which hide leaks for days.

Water follows gravity. It runs down the back of the cabinet, under the toe kick and into the flooring layers, where it spreads far wider than the spot you can see.

Why cabinets are the hard part

Particle board bases

Many cabinet boxes are particle board with a thin laminate skin. Once wet, they swell, crumble at the edges and rarely shrink back.

Solid wood and plywood

These dry far better. A plywood box that was wet briefly can often be dried in place.

Toe kicks

The strip at the bottom hides a gap where water pools. We remove it and drill small holes to let air reach the cavity.

What stays inside

Cleaners, pots and paper stored under the sink soak up water and keep the cabinet damp. Empty everything.

Floors under and around the cabinets

Floor typeWhat usually happens
Ceramic or porcelain tileTile itself is fine, but the grout, mortar bed and subfloor underneath may stay wet
Vinyl sheet or plankWater gets under seams and edges and stays trapped beneath the surface
Engineered or solid woodBoards cup and gap, and may need to come up to dry the subfloor
LaminateSwells at the seams and generally cannot be saved once the core is wet

The flooring that runs under the cabinets is often the part nobody sees. When it is wet, the cabinets may need to be dried or lifted so air can reach it.

Counters, appliances and what to move

Granite and quartz tops are not harmed by water, but the particle board under them and the sink base can be. Laminate counters have a core that swells at the edges and around the sink cutout.

Pull the dishwasher forward if you can do it safely, and keep the refrigerator from sitting in a wet area. Tell us about appliances so we do not run into them.

Kitchen water safety steps for the first hour

Shut the valve

Close the angle stop under the sink, the dishwasher valve or the ice maker line. If you cannot find it, close the main.

Unplug nearby appliances

Disposals, dishwashers and refrigerators near standing water are an electrical risk. Cut the breaker if the panel is dry.

Empty the cabinet

Take out everything stored in the wet base so air can move and nothing stains the shelf.

Wipe and photograph

Take photos first, then blot standing water with towels. Do not leave wet rags on the floor.

Keep the air moving

Open the cabinet doors and run a fan on the floor, but do not rely on it for deeper drying.

How a kitchen job runs

1

Stop and extract

Confirm the source is off, then pull standing water from the floor and cabinet base.

2

Open up

Remove toe kicks, drill access holes and lift wet flooring where needed.

3

Measure

Use a moisture meter and thermal camera to find wet cabinet backs, drywall and subfloor.

4

Dry and decide

Run air movers and a dehumidifier, then check readings daily to decide what stays.

The work follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration. If the dishwasher or other unit was the cause, see appliance leak water damage. Wet flooring in the adjoining rooms is covered under carpet water damage.

Insurance for kitchen leaks

A burst supply line or a sudden dishwasher failure is often treated as sudden and accidental, while a slow drip under the sink for months often is not. Policies differ, so check your policy and ask your insurer what is covered.

Avoid these mistakes and prevent the next leak

  • Setting a fan outside the cabinet and ignoring the inside
  • Covering wet flooring with a rug or a new layer
  • Using bleach on wet particle board
  • Waiting a week to see if the swelling goes down
  • Skipping a check of braided supply lines for bulges or rust

Common questions

Can I call about kitchen water damage at night or on a weekend in La Palma, CA?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open all hours. Shut the supply valve to the leaking fixture, then call.

Can wet cabinets be dried in place?

Sometimes. Plywood and solid wood often can be, while swollen particle board usually cannot. We check readings before deciding.

Why do you remove the toe kick?

It hides a gap where water pools. Removing it and drilling small holes lets air reach the cabinet base and the floor beneath.

What if the floor under the cabinets is wet?

We may need to lift the flooring at the edges or detach cabinets to dry the subfloor properly.

Will my granite counter be damaged?

Stone is usually fine, but the cabinet base and any particle board under it can be. Laminate tops can swell at the edges.

Do you replace the cabinets?

Our work is drying and removal. Cabinet replacement and flooring installation are rebuild trades you can hire once the area is dry.
