Honest context

Storm water damage in a place where big storms are uncommon

Southern California storms are less frequent than in many parts of the country, but they can be intense. A home built for dry weather often has little margin when hard, wind-driven rain arrives.

Water then enters through gaps nobody tested, such as door thresholds, window frames, garage doors, attic vents and aging roofs.

Why storm water is treated as dirty water

Water that enters from outside crosses soil, roofs, gutters and pavement before it reaches your floor. It carries dirt, oils and organic matter, so we do not treat it as clean supply-line water.

The published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard defines water categories from clean to contaminated, and outdoor water is usually handled with extra care. The flood damage restoration page covers contaminated water in detail.

Ways storm water reaches the inside

Door and window gaps

Wind pushes rain sideways into thresholds and frames. Look for wet carpet at the base of sliding glass doors.

Roof and attic openings

Water enters at flashing, vents and worn roofing and travels into ceilings. See roof leak water damage for that path.

Clogged gutters and drains

A blocked gutter overflows against the wall. A blocked yard drain lets water stand against the house.

Low yard and garage thresholds

Water pools where the ground slopes toward the house and flows under a garage door or into a slab edge.

Low ground, elevation and what pools

La Palma sits at about 46 feet above sea level, according to public reference sources, which is low and flat. Flat ground drains slowly, so water that pools against a house can sit for hours.

Walk the outside of your home after a storm. Look at where downspouts discharge, whether soil slopes toward the walls and whether patio drains are clear.

Safety steps when storm water is in the house

Electrical hazards

Do not stand in water near outlets, power strips or a garage panel. If water reached them, shut off the main breaker only if the panel and floor are dry.

Contaminated contact

Wear boots and gloves. Keep children and pets out until the area is cleaned.

Slip and structure risk

Wet tile, concrete and stairs are slick. Do not walk through standing water that could hide holes or debris.

Document first

Photograph water lines on walls and furniture before moving anything, and note the time you found the water.

How a storm water job moves

1

Extract

Pumps and wet vacuums remove standing water from floors and garages.

2

Remove

Soaked carpet pad, baseboard and wet drywall bottoms are removed if contaminated.

3

Clean and sanitize

Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with antimicrobial products.

4

Dry

Air movers and dehumidifiers run with daily moisture readings until dry.

After the dry-out, repairs fall under water damage repair. If the water sat a few days, mold remediation may need to be part of the plan.

Wet garages are common. Concrete slabs dry slowly, and stored cardboard boxes soak up water and hold it, so sorting stored items early helps.

Water that is not rain: groundwater and the aquifer

The Santa Ana River recharges the Orange County Water District aquifer, which supplies part of the area's groundwater, according to the district. That recharge is managed upstream, so it does not by itself tell you whether your street will pool.

Some La Palma homes are billed by the city and others by Golden State Water. Your bill shows which one is yours, and that provider is the call to make before ours if a main breaks or a yard line floods.

Is storm water covered, and is it a flood?

Insurers often draw a line between rain entering through a storm-damaged roof and surface water entering from the ground. Flood usually requires separate flood coverage, so check your policy and ask your insurer which one applies.

Check the FEMA flood map for your address to see how your lot is mapped. Photos, dates and a list of damaged items make any claim easier to process.

What affects the cost of storm water cleanup

  • How deep the water was and how long it stood
  • Whether carpet pad, drywall or insulation must be removed
  • The water category and the cleaning it requires
  • The number of rooms and the garage involved
  • Drying days and equipment count
  • Access for pumps and vehicles

Mistakes to avoid and ways to prepare

  • Wading through water near live outlets
  • Using household vacuums on wet carpet
  • Leaving soaked cardboard and fabric in a closed garage
  • Waiting until the storm passes to start photographing
  • Letting gutters and yard drains clog before the rainy season

Common questions

Is emergency storm water damage available in La Palma, CA?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open during and after storms, including nights and weekends. We can talk through safe steps while a crew is arranged.

Is storm water considered flood water?

Often the two are treated differently by insurers. Rain through a roof or window is storm damage, while surface water rising from the ground is usually flood.

Does homeowners insurance cover storm water?

It depends on the policy and how the water entered. Check your policy and ask your insurer, because flood usually needs separate coverage.

Can I clean storm water myself?

Small amounts on hard floors, yes, with gloves. Carpet, insulation and anything that stood in water for a day should be handled by a restoration crew.

What should I check outside after a storm?

Look at gutters, downspouts, yard drains and soil slope near the walls. These show where water can come in next time.

Do you repair roofs or windows?

No. Roofers and window contractors handle those. We dry, clean and restore what water damaged.
