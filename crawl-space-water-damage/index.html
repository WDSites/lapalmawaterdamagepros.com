Before you read on

Not every La Palma home has a crawl space

Crawl spaces are less common here than concrete slabs, and only homes with a raised foundation have one. If yours sits directly on concrete, the slab leak page is the relevant one.

You can tell by looking for an access hatch in a closet or hallway floor, or foundation vents along the outside wall. If you are unsure, your inspection report or permit records will say. This page is for those that do have the space under the floor.

Where the water under a house comes from

A drain line or supply pipe can leak into the dirt, a water heater or washer can overflow onto the floor and run down through gaps, and surface water can enter through vents. Each source leaves different wet materials behind.

The ground itself matters too. La Palma sits at about 46 feet of elevation, according to published geographic data, so the ground is fairly level. Water under a floor has little slope to leave by, and a wet crawl space can stay wet for weeks if the source is not found.

What a wet crawl space shows you

What you noticeWhat it usually means
Standing water on the soilA plumbing leak, a drainage problem or water entering at the vents
Insulation sagging from the joistsBatts have absorbed water and are now heavy and useless
Dark, wet soil and a damp smellMoisture has been present for some time
Torn or pooled vapor barrierPlastic sheeting is holding water or no longer covering the soil
Springy, stained or cupped floors aboveSubfloor and flooring are taking in water from below
Musty odor in the roomsAir from the crawl space is moving up into the living area

Any one of these is worth a look. Several together mean the space needs to be dried, not just pumped.

Crawl space safety before anyone goes in

It is a confined space

Low clearance, tight turns and one way out. Never go in alone, and tell someone outside where you are.

Assume wires are live

Water and extension cords or junction boxes do not mix. Turn off power to the area at the panel first if you can do so dry.

Protect your skin and lungs

Wear gloves, boots, eye protection and a respirator rated for dust. Old insulation and soil can be irritating.

Watch for pests and sharp debris

Nails, glass and rodents are common under old homes.

Stop the source

Close the main shutoff or the appliance valve. A pump cannot win against a running supply line.

What happens when we dry a crawl space

1

Pump

Place submersible pumps at the low points and move water out through hoses.

2

Remove

Pull wet insulation, torn plastic and loose debris, bag it and carry it out.

3

Assess

Probe joists and subfloor with a moisture meter and check the soil.

4

Dry

Set air movers and a dehumidifier in the space and read the meters each day.

The method follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, which anyone can ask a company about. Where the floor above is also wet, structural drying covers how the wood is brought back to dry.

Odor, mold risk and the vapor barrier

Wet wood and soil start to smell within days, and the smell travels upward through gaps around pipes and the floor. The EPA ties mold growth to materials that stay damp beyond a day or two, which is why fast pumping matters.

If the water has been sitting longer, the mold remediation page explains what that work involves. Torn or soaked vapor barrier is removed, and reinstalling it afterward is a separate step once the soil is dry.

What we do and what we hand to others

We do not do waterproofing, grading, French drains or sump systems. Those are drainage and foundation contractors, and they are the right call if surface water keeps coming in.

We handle the damage: water removal, wet insulation and debris, drying of the framing and subfloor, and readings that show it is dry. Your plumber fixes any pipe that caused it.

Insurance and an old neighborhood

Sudden and accidental water from a failed supply line is often covered, while seepage and surface water usually are not. Flood generally needs separate flood coverage, so check the FEMA flood map for your address and your policy. Ask your insurer before work starts.

Mistakes to avoid and quick prevention

  • Going in without checking power and ventilation
  • Letting wet insulation hang because the water has drained
  • Closing the vents tight and trapping damp air
  • Running a household fan down there and calling it dry
  • Skipping a recheck after the next rain

Common questions

Is emergency crawl space water damage available in La Palma, CA?

Yes. The 24/7 dispatch line is open nights and weekends. Stay out of the space until power is safe, then call.

Does my home have a crawl space?

Only homes with a raised foundation do. Look for an access hatch and foundation vents, or check your inspection report or permit records.

Do you waterproof or regrade?

No. Drainage and foundation contractors do waterproofing and grading. We remove water and wet materials and dry the structure.

Can wet insulation be saved?

Rarely. Once fiberglass batts have soaked and fallen, they hold water against the wood and are usually removed.

Is it safe to go under the house myself?

Only with care. It is confined, wiring may be live and the air can be dusty, so many people prefer a crew.

Will the smell go away after drying?

Often it fades once wet materials are out and the space is dry. Lingering odor may mean hidden moisture or mold that needs a closer look.
