What to do before we arrive in Stanton if water is still spreading

Shut it off

Golden State Water serves Stanton. If the leak is at the meter, call them. Inside, close the fixture valve or the main shutoff.

Tell the manager

In an apartment, notify the property manager so neighbors can be warned and the building shutoff can be used.

Stay clear of wiring

If water touches outlets, stay out and call the utility or 911 for any electrical risk.

Lift and photograph

Move valuables to dry ground and photograph the damage before anything is removed.
