Owners and renters in Buena Park

About 54 percent of Buena Park housing units are owner-occupied and about 46 percent are rented, according to 2020 census data. In a city with that many renters, water damage calls often come from tenants who need to involve a landlord.

If you rent, tell the owner in writing, photograph the damage and your belongings, and keep copies. If you own, document the loss before cleanup and ask your insurer what your policy covers. Either way, the 24/7 line can start extraction while the paperwork sorts itself out.

What to do before we arrive in Buena Park if water is still spreading

Stop the water

The City of Buena Park runs its own water utility. If the leak is at the meter, call the city. If it is inside, close the shutoff at the fixture or the main valve.

Stay safe

If water reaches outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911. Cut power only from a dry, safe position.

Move belongings

Lift electronics, papers and rugs off the floor. Pad furniture legs with foil.

Keep clear

Water that came from a drain, a toilet or outside should be treated as contaminated.

Rain, runoff and the foothills side of town

Coyote Creek runs through Buena Park, and a Tongva village named Juyubit once stood beside it at the foot of the West Coyote Hills. That is history, not a flood forecast, but it is a reason to look at your own lot.
