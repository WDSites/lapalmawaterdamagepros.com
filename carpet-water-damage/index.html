Quick answer

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often yes, when the water was clean and the carpet is extracted and dried quickly. Not when the water was contaminated. The deciding factor is the water category, then how long the carpet stayed wet, then what is underneath it.

Carpet is a surface layer. The pad below acts as a sponge, and the subfloor under that holds the water longest. All three layers matter.

Reading the carpet after water

Squishes underfoot

The pad is full of water. Extraction is needed now, and lifting the carpet corner helps check the floor.

Looks dry, feels cool

A damp pad or subfloor under a dry surface. A meter will read what the eye cannot.

Musty smell after a day

Moisture is trapped in the pad or the edge by the wall. Odor means the job is not done.

Stains climbing the wall

Wicking. The wall paper or drywall is pulling water upward from the carpet edge.

Water category decides what happens

Water sourceCategoryCarpet and pad

Categories are defined in the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. For a drain backup, see sewage backup cleanup.

Safety before you touch wet carpet

Check the breaker

Wet carpet near outlets and floor cords is a shock risk. Turn off power to the room if you can reach the panel dry.

Know the source

Shut off the supply valve. If the water came from a drain, do not walk through it barefoot.

Lift valuables

Put aluminum foil or plastic blocks under furniture legs so they do not stain the carpet.

Watch your footing

Wet carpet over tile or concrete is slippery. Keep children away from stretched or lifted areas.

How carpet is extracted

A truck mount unit sits outside and runs hoses to a wand with strong suction and heated water. A portable unit does the same in a compact size. Both pull water from the fibers before drying starts.

A weighted extraction wand or a floating tool pulls water through the pad. Pulling more water with extraction means less moisture left for dehumidifiers to remove.

Floating the carpet to dry the pad

1

Release

The carpet is pulled from the tack strip at one wall, never the full room.

2

Lift

The loose section is raised so air can move under it. Pad is checked and removed if it is damaged.

3

Dry

Air movers are set to blow under the carpet while a dehumidifier lowers the room humidity.

4

Restretch

When the subfloor reads dry, the carpet goes back on the strip, restretched with a power stretcher.

Carpet that has been floated and restretched usually looks like it did before. Tack strip that has been wet may need replacement, and the subfloor is checked against dry readings first. The drying science is on the structural drying page.

When replacement beats drying

Replacing a section is often cheaper than repeated drying attempts. Carpet and pad that are replaced can be matched to the room, and the crew can tell you before work begins.

  • The water was category 3
  • The carpet delaminated or the backing came apart
  • The pad is crumbled, stained or smells after extraction
  • The carpet sat wet for several days
  • The subfloor stays wet under it and cannot be dried while it is covered

Mistakes to avoid with wet carpet

  • Using a household vacuum on soaked carpet
  • Running only a floor fan and leaving the pad wet
  • Covering the area with a rug
  • Using heat to speed drying, which can set stains
  • Waiting a week before calling

Common questions

Can I call about carpet water damage at night or on a weekend in La Palma, CA?

Yes. The 24/7 line is open at night, on weekends and on holidays. Start by stopping the water and lifting furniture off the carpet.

Does the pad always need to be replaced?

No, but often. Pad can sometimes be saved after clean water if it is reached fast, though it is cheap to replace next to the cost of a failed dry.

Why does my carpet feel dry but smell musty?

Moisture is probably in the pad or the subfloor. The surface fibers dry first, so the problem hides below.

Can wet carpet cause damage to walls?

Yes. Water wicks into the base of drywall and baseboard. A meter reading at the wall base shows how far it went.

How long does it take carpet to dry?

Often a few days with extraction, air movers and a dehumidifier. Thick pad and wet subfloor take longer.

Will the carpet shrink or stretch?

It can loosen when wet. A power stretcher restores it, as long as the backing is intact.
