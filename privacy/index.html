What this policy covers

This policy explains how La Palma Water Damage Pros, at 1 Centerpointe Dr, La Palma, CA 90623, handles information when you visit this website or call our line. The site has no forms, so we do not collect information through web forms.

Information we may collect

  • Phone numbers and call details when you call us

  • Your email address and message if you email us

How we use it

We use call information to respond to your request, arrange service, improve quality and keep records. We do not use your information for anything unrelated to providing and improving our service.

We share information with the restoration professionals who perform your job, and with others only when needed to provide service or when the law requires it.
