Utilities in Anaheim are different

Anaheim Public Utilities is the only municipal water and electric utility in Orange County. For many addresses that means one city office handles both water and electricity, which is useful when a leak affects electrical safety.

The city's La Palma Water Complex, dedicated in 2018 with a four-million-gallon reservoir, a well and a pump station, serves Central and West Anaheim. Check your bill to confirm your provider before calling about a meter leak.

What to do before we arrive in Anaheim: if water is still spreading

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff. A leak at the meter goes to your water provider.

Electrical safety

If water touches outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911.

Lift valuables

Move electronics, documents and small furniture to a dry area.

Keep clear

Water from drains, toilets or outside is contaminated. Avoid contact.

Neighborhoods and properties we see in Anaheim

Anaheim ranges from the historic Anaheim Colony district downtown to apartments and homes near the Platinum Triangle and the Honda Center. Older buildings often bring older plumbing, which is worth knowing when a supply line fails.

Multi-unit buildings add a layer of coordination, because water from one unit can damage another. Tell the property manager right away and photograph both units. For a drying plan, see water mitigation, and for rebuilding, water damage repair.

Older homes near the Anaheim Colony

Anaheim was founded in 1857 by German families and incorporated in 1876, so it contains some of the oldest housing in this part of Orange County. Older buildings may have original galvanized or cast iron pipes, plaster walls and layered flooring.

When water gets into an older wall, the first step is finding what the wall is made of. Ask before disturbing old ceiling texture or old flooring, because some mid-century materials contained asbestos and old paint can contain lead. A lab test comes before demolition.

Commercial and resort-area properties
