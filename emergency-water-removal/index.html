When standing water is an emergency

If water is pooled on the floor in La Palma, treat it as an emergency, because it soaks into drywall, subfloor and furniture every hour it stays. Emergency water removal is the first job: pumps and extraction units pull the bulk of the water out so drying can begin.

Our 24/7 line takes calls at night, on weekends and on holidays. While you wait, stop the source if you can, shut off the main water valve, lift valuables off the floor, and keep people and pets out of the water. After removal comes water mitigation, where air movers and dehumidifiers finish the job.

What to do before we arrive: your first ten minutes

Stop the source

Close the valve at the fixture or the main water valve. Use the meter shutoff if the interior valve is stuck.

Mind the electricity

Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. If water touches outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911 first.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, shoes, books and small furniture to dry ground. Put foil or wood blocks under furniture legs.

Keep people and pets out

Water from a toilet, drain or outside is contaminated. Do not walk through it or run a household vacuum on it.

How water extraction works

Crews use submersible pumps for deep water and truck-mounted or portable extraction units for carpet, pad and hard floors. Weighted extraction wands press water out of carpet fibers, and the pad is pulled when it cannot be dried in place.

Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras then show where water traveled behind baseboards and into walls. Water you cannot see is the water that causes the mold, so the survey is as important as the pumping.

  • Submersible pumps move standing water
  • Extraction units pull water from carpet, pad and hard floors
  • Moisture meters find wet material
  • Thermal imaging cameras trace hidden water
  • Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers take over for drying

Burst pipe water damage

A burst pipe sends water through a wall or ceiling at a high rate until the valve is closed. The first move is the main shutoff, then power to the affected rooms if the panel is dry and reachable.

The water is clean at first, which is Category 1, and that is the best time to save materials. Left standing for a day or two, it can degrade toward Category 2, so fast extraction protects more of your home. We cut small inspection holes in the wall when needed, extract, and start drying the cavity so the burst does not become a rebuild.

Common causes are supply lines at toilets and sinks, washing machine hoses, water heater connections and aging pipe joints. Replace rubber washer hoses and old supply lines before they fail, because a burst under pressure moves a lot of water in a short time.

Water category decides the plan

CategorySourceWhat it means
Category 1Clean supply line or sink overflowMost materials can be dried and saved if acted on quickly
Category 2Dishwasher or washing machine discharge, aged clean waterCarpet pad is usually removed and surfaces are cleaned
Category 3Sewage, rising outside waterPorous materials are removed and the area is sanitized

These categories come from the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration. Ask any company whether its crews work to it. Category 3 water also means the flood damage restoration page applies.

What sets the price of water removal

The price follows the volume of water, how deep it is, the water category, the area and materials affected, and how many days of drying follow. A shallow Category 1 spill is a smaller job than a flooded floor of a house.

We explain scope and price before work starts, and the real number comes from seeing the damage on site. For insurance, sudden and accidental water damage is often covered while gradual leaks often are not. Check your policy and photograph the water before it is removed. Common questions

Do you handle water removal emergencies at night or on weekends in La Palma?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, and a dispatcher walks you through the first steps while crews are arranged.

What should I do while I wait for the crew?

Stop the source or close the main valve, keep people and pets out of the water, lift valuables, and only cut power if the panel is dry and safe to reach.

Can wet carpet be saved after extraction?

Often yes when the water was clean and dried quickly. The pad is usually replaced, and contaminated water means carpet is removed.

How do I know if the water is safe to touch?

Clean supply line water is the safest, but any water from a toilet, drain or outside should be treated as contaminated. When unsure, stay out of it.
