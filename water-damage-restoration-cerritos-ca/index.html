What to do before we arrive in Cerritos if water is still spreading

Shut it off

Close the fixture valve or the main shutoff. If the leak is at the meter, the City of Cerritos water utility is the call.

Mind the power

Stay out of rooms where water reaches outlets, and call the utility or 911 for electrical or gas risk.

Protect belongings

Lift papers, electronics and rugs. Place foil or wood blocks under furniture legs.

Skip the household vacuum

Do not use it on wet floors or sewage. Wait for extraction equipment.
